About Annual Giving

A Commitment Across Generations

For more than six decades, IIT Kanpur has been shaped by generations of students, faculty, alumni and supporters. The Institute we know today reflects their collective commitment to building something that would endure beyond their own time.

Annual Giving offers our community a meaningful way to carry this commitment forward. Through contributions made each year, we help IIT Kanpur support its students and faculty, respond to emerging needs and advance research and innovation.

As part of Greater Than Us – The IIT Kanpur Campaign, Annual Giving connects individual contributions to a larger institutional purpose. Every gift, regardless of its size, helps carry forward what earlier generations built and create new possibilities for those who follow.

Student support

A Timely Gift, A Stronger Future

Coming from a small farming family in rural Uttar Pradesh, Anshika knew early what financial uncertainty felt like. While a tuition fee waiver helped her enter IIT Kanpur, daily expenses like mess bills, electricity charges, and a failing laptop became overwhelming. At a critical moment, alumni support arrived. Pending dues were cleared, her laptop was repaired, and for the first time in months, Anshika could focus fully on her academics and placement preparation. This timely support restored not just stability, but confidence. Inspired by alumni generosity, she now dreams of giving back just as she was supported.

Anshika Singh
B.Tech, IITK Student
Global excellence

From Campus to Global Champions

When Team Aerial Robotics qualified for the global finals at ICUAS 2025 in the United States, travel costs stood between them and the world stage. Alumni stepped in. With support from the IITK Alumni Association (Outer Delhi Chapter), the team represented IIT Kanpur internationally and returned as global winners. Competing against top teams from across continents, their first-place finish showcased what becomes possible when alumni invest in student ambition. This victory is a powerful reminder: alumni support doesn't just fund travel, it fuels global excellence and institutional pride.

Team Aerial Robotics
Winners, ICUAS 2025
Community health

A Clearer Vision for Brighter Futures

For many schoolchildren, unclear vision can become an invisible barrier to learning. Through Project Vision, a DoRA-supported initiative led by CDAP and enabled by alumni contributions, children at Kendriya Vidyalayas received access to vision screening and medical support. Across two phases, 150+ children were screened, helping identify vision impairments early. Children who needed spectacles received them, making it easier to read, write and participate in class. One student shared how getting spectacles helped them see the blackboard clearly and study comfortably. A simple intervention made a meaningful difference to everyday learning.

Project Vision
Academic engagement

Celebrating 50 Years Through Academic Impact

During its Golden Jubilee year, the Department of Management Sciences (DoMS), with support channelled through the DoRA Office, turned a milestone celebration into sustained academic engagement. Alumni contributions enabled 70 academic engagements, including 38 talks and seminars, 7 workshops and 25 webinars through the VIBGYOR and Kaleidoscope series. These engagements strengthened industry-academia collaboration, expanded national and international partnerships, and enriched learning opportunities for students and faculty. As Prof. B. V. Phani, Head of DoMS, noted, alumni engagement rekindled a strong sense of camaraderie between the department and its alumni, inspiring continued participation in its growth.

Department of Management Sciences
Scholarship

A Dream Called Aerospace, A Future Taking Flight

From a state-board school in Hyderabad to IIT Kanpur, Ananthula Abhivardhan’s journey has been driven by a dream to contribute to aerospace research and development. Now pursuing a dual degree in Aerospace Engineering, he faced the challenge of managing the financial demands of an IIT education. Alumni support eased this burden, allowing him to focus on academics, explore new opportunities and make the most of his time at IIT Kanpur. From late-night conversations and new friendships to earning a bronze medal with the Inter IIT team, his journey has been shaped by experiences that extend far beyond the classroom. With the right support, Abhivardhan is moving closer to a future he once only imagined, proving how a scholarship can do more than ease financial pressure. It can give a dream the freedom to take flight.

Ananthula Abhivardhan
Dual Degree, Aerospace Engineering
